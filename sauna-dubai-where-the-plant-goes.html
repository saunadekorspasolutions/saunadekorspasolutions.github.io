A Sauna in Dubai: Where the Plant Goes

Updated: 7 November 2026

Short answer: in a Dubai sauna only the heater, the stones, the temperature sensor and the light fittings belong inside the hot room. The power unit or contactor box, the control panel, the isolator, the light drivers, any extract fan and the shower valves go outside it, in a dry, cool place a technician can open. Where that place is depends on whether the sauna sits in an apartment, a villa, a garden or a hotel. Sauna Dekor Spa Solutions marks it on the layout before the walls go up.

A Finnish sauna runs at 80–100 °C. Electronics placed in or above that heat fail early, and in Dubai the space around the sauna is often hot as well.

What is the plant behind a sauna?

A sauna has little plant compared with a steam room or a pool, but each item has a fixed rule. We have used EOS heaters for about 20 years, and the distances and cable sizes come from the heater maker's manual, not from habit.

Where does it go in a Dubai apartment?

Apartments rarely have a plant room. The power unit and drivers usually go in a utility cupboard next to the sauna or in the wall of an adjoining bathroom, behind a panel that unscrews. The building's approval is needed for the electrical connection and for any extract to the outside, and the management office often sets the working hours for the installation.

Do not use the false ceiling above the sauna for drivers. In many apartments that void is shared with air-conditioning ducts, and condensation dripping from a duct onto a driver is a fault we see.

Where does it go in a villa?

A villa gives more choice: a utility room, a basement plant room or a technical cupboard in the spa corridor. Keep the power unit within the cable distance the heater manual allows, keep the panel beside the sauna door, and put the isolator where the person who services the sauna can find it without asking.

Under a roof in Dubai summer, an attic void can be far hotter than the rooms below. That is not a place for a power unit or drivers, even if it is the easiest place to run cables.

What about a sauna in the garden?

An outdoor sauna in Dubai sits in sun for most of the day. The control panel and power unit go in a shaded, ventilated enclosure or inside the house wall nearest the sauna, never on the sunny face of the cabin. Outdoor electrical parts need a protection rating suited to the location, which the electrician sets from the maker's data. Plan a drain for the shower and a covered route for the cable from the house.

How is a hotel different?

A hotel sauna usually has a real plant room, but it may be far from the sauna. The power unit then goes in a technical cupboard near the sauna rather than in the main plant room, so the heater cable stays within the maker's limit. The panel is often placed at the spa reception or in a staff corridor, with a simple display beside the door for guests. Access for staff without passing through the wet area matters more than in a villa.

Frequently asked questions

Can the control panel go inside the sauna?

Only a model built for it. Most panels go outside the door, where they stay cool and can be read without letting heat out.

How far can the power unit be from the sauna?

As far as the heater maker's manual allows for the cable size used. The electrician confirms it from that data.

Does a small apartment sauna need a cupboard for plant?

Usually a small one. A single heater with a built-in contactor needs less, but the drivers and isolator still need a place outside the hot room.

Who decides where the plant goes?
