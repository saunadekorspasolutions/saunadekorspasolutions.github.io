Steam generator for a Dubai villa: what to send the supplier before ordering

Updated: 3 November 2026

Short answer: a steam generator is sized from six inputs: room volume, wall and ceiling build-up, glass area, door type, how quickly the room must heat up, and how often it runs. Send those six with the room drawing, and the supplier can choose a generator from the maker's table instead of guessing. A steam room works at 40–50 °C at close to 100 % relative humidity, so an undersized generator never reaches that point.

What are the six inputs?

InputWhy it changes the size
Room volume (length × width × height)more air to bring to temperature and saturate
Wall and ceiling build-uptile on concrete or stone absorbs much more heat than tile on insulated board
Glass areaglass loses heat and condenses steam quickly
Doora full glass door adds to the glass area and to leakage
Heat-up time wanteda faster warm-up needs more output
Use patterna hotel room running all day differs from a villa room used in the evening

Generator makers publish tables that turn these inputs into a model. The most common mistake is giving only the room volume: a stone-clad room with a glass front can need a far larger generator than a tiled board room of the same size.

What does the generator cupboard need?

  1. Space and access to open the unit for descaling and service.
  2. Water supply with an isolation valve; in hard-water areas, softening or a descaling routine.
  3. Drain for the automatic flush and the safety outlet.
  4. Power from the maker's datasheet, on its own isolator.
  5. Ventilation of the cupboard, so heat from the unit does not build up.
  6. A short steam line to the room, falling towards the room or the drain so condensate does not collect.

In Dubai villas the cupboard is often placed in a plant room or a utility space next to the bathroom. The shorter and better insulated the steam line, the less output is lost before the steam reaches the room.

What should be agreed for the steam room itself?

The sensor position, the steam outlet position and the control panel position are drawn together. The outlet sits low and away from the bench, so nobody sits in front of it; the sensor sits where it reads the room, not the outlet. The ceiling is sloped so condensate runs to the walls, and the floor falls to a drain. A fragrance pump, if wanted, is planned with the generator from the start.

Which documents should come back from the supplier?

  • the generator datasheet with output, power supply and water connections
  • the sizing calculation or table reference used for the choice
  • the control panel and sensor wiring diagram
  • the service and descaling routine

Keep these four with the handover file. They are what a service engineer asks for first.
