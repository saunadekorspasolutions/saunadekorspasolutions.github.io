What Does a Spa Developer Do on a Dubai Project?

Updated: 11 November 2026

Short answer: a spa developer takes a Dubai spa from an empty floor to rooms in daily use, through 5 stages: brief, zoning, technical design, build, and commissioning with the first months of operation. Sauna Dekor Spa Solutions works across all 5 stages as a manufacturer, with 40 years of building wet and thermal rooms since 1987. Depending on scope, delivery runs from about 2 weeks for a single sauna to up to 6 months for large projects.

Stage 1: what goes into the brief?

The brief turns a business idea into a room list. It records who the spa is for (hotel guests, residents, members or a private family), how many people use it at peak hour, which thermal rooms are wanted and what floor area is available. The brief is signed off in writing before stage 2 starts, because every later drawing is checked against it.

Stage 2: how is the spa zoned?

Zoning sets the order a guest walks through. A developer groups the rooms into 4 zones and keeps the wet ones together, so drains and vapour stay in one part of the building.

ZoneRoomsWhy it sits where it does
Arrival and changingReception, lockers, showersDry floor, near the entrance
ThermalSauna, steam room, hammamShared plant, short pipe runs
ColdCold plunge pool, ice fountain, experience showersNext to the hot room doors
RestRelaxation area, treatment roomsQuiet side, away from plant noise

Stage 3: what does technical design fix?

Technical design fixes everything the building has to deliver to the spa: power to each heater and generator, water and drain points, extract air, ceiling voids and the plant room. In our projects every thermal room gets 1 section drawing and 1 MEP points drawing, checked against the architect's plan before the MEP design is frozen.

Stage 4: who builds the rooms?

The rooms are built by the spa developer's own team or by a specialist contractor working to its drawings. In our case, saunas, benches, doors and hammam pieces are made in our Istanbul workshop, and the 19 people on our Istanbul team support the installation team on site. Steam generators come from Condair and sauna heaters from EOS; we do not manufacture them.

Stage 5: what happens at commissioning and after opening?

Commissioning proves each room reaches its design value: 80–100 °C in a Finnish sauna and 40–50 °C at close to 100 % relative humidity in a steam room. Staff are trained on the controls, and the handover file carries as-built drawings, manuals and the maintenance calendar.

Pools are checked the same way. For the water of a whirlpool or spa pool, a typical working value is 30 °C.

After opening, the first months show how the spa is really used. We keep spare parts for the equipment we install in stock in Istanbul, so a fault in a steam generator or heater is fixed without waiting for a new order.

Frequently asked questions

Is a spa developer the same as a spa contractor?

Not exactly. A contractor builds to drawings it receives; a developer also shapes the brief, the zoning and the technical design. Some firms, including ours, do both, so design and construction come from 1 team.

When should a spa developer join a Dubai project?

At concept stage, before the architect freezes the floor plan. The plant room, drains and ceiling voids are fixed early, and moving them after the MEP design is issued costs weeks on site and often changes the finishes as well.

Can a spa developer show the rooms before they are built?

Yes. We use our own 3D models of saunas, steam rooms and hammams, so owners see bench heights, door swings and lighting before approval, alongside the section drawings for each room.

What warranty does the client receive?

The same three terms apply on every project: a 24-month warranty on workmanship and manufacturing, a 24-month manufacturer's warranty on electrical and mechanical equipment, and a 5-year warranty on structure and waterproofing.

Where is the work managed from?

Our Dubai office in Latifa Tower, World Trade Centre, manages projects in the UAE. Manufacturing stays in our own workshop in Istanbul.
