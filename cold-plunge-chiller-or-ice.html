Cold Plunge: Chiller or Ice?

Updated: 12 November 2026

Short answer: Sauna Dekor Spa Solutions builds cold plunge pools for water temperatures from 2 to 15 °C, and almost all of them are held at that temperature by a chiller, not by ice. Ice cools a small tub for one session and then melts into the water. A chiller holds the set point every day, together with filtration and water treatment. Ice still has a place for an occasional plunge at home.

How does ice cool a plunge?

Bags of ice are added to the water before a session. They cool it quickly, then melt, and the water warms again within hours. Every session needs more ice, the melt dilutes any water treatment and the level rises. For a single person using a small tub now and then, that can be enough. For a hotel, a club or a daily routine at home, it is not practical.

How does a chiller work?

A chiller is a small refrigeration unit that pulls heat out of the water and rejects it to the air. It runs together with a circulation pump and a filter, so the water is cooled, filtered and treated in one loop. The controller holds the set point the owner chooses within the range the plunge is built for.

What does a chiller need from the building?

  1. A position close to the plunge, so the pipe runs are short.
  2. Free air around it, because it rejects heat while it works. A chiller in a closed cupboard fights its own warm air.
  3. A dedicated electrical supply sized for the unit.
  4. A drain and service access for maintenance and for changing the water.
  5. Protection from direct sun where it sits outdoors in a hot climate.

What else decides how well it works?

The shell. An insulated vessel and a cover reduce the heat the chiller has to remove. A plunge in direct sun, or sitting in a warm room without insulation, needs a larger chiller to hold the same temperature. Filtration and water treatment matter as much as temperature: cold water still needs to be kept clean.

When does ice still make sense?

For an occasional plunge at home, in a portable tub, where installing power and a drain is not possible. It also has a role as a quick top-up for events. For any plunge used every day, a chiller is the reliable choice.

Frequently asked questions

What temperature range can a cold plunge be built for?

We build cold plunge pools for water temperatures from 2 to 15 °C; the set point within that range is the owner's choice.

Does a chiller work outdoors in the Gulf?

Yes, if it is sized for the real outdoor temperatures, shaded and given free air around it.
