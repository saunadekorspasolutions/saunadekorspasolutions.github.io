A Cold Plunge Pool: Where the Plant Goes

Updated: 13 November 2026

Short answer: at Sauna Dekor Spa Solutions we put cold plunge pool plant in one dry, ventilated, lockable space as close to the pool as the chiller and pump makers allow: chiller, circulation pump, filter, water treatment and control panel together, with a floor drain, a water point and a clear path for service. The one exception is an air-cooled chiller, which needs outside air of its own. The plant position is fixed before the pool shell is ordered, because pipe routes, drain falls and the electrical feed all start from it.

We build cold plunge pools for water temperatures from 2 to 15 °C. The plant is what holds the pool there, day after day, and every hour it runs depends on where it sits.

What is in the plant, piece by piece?

  • Chiller: cools the pool water. Air-cooled units reject heat to outside air; water-cooled units reject it to a building water circuit.
  • Circulation pump: moves water from the pool through filter, treatment and chiller, and back.
  • Filter: cartridge or sand, with a backwash or rinse line to drain if it is a sand filter.
  • Water treatment: UV, dosing or both, with any chemical store kept apart from electrics.
  • Control panel and isolator: set point, timers, alarms, and a local switch a technician can reach.
  • Balance tank: only for an overflow or deck-level pool, and placed lower than the pool edge.
  • Drain and water point: for emptying, backwash, rinsing and topping up.

How far from the pool can the plant sit?

As close as the room plan allows, and never further than the chiller and pump makers' data says. Long pipe runs add heat gain on the way and friction in the pipes, and both cost cooling. Pipes between pool and plant are insulated along their whole length; where they cross warm spaces, the insulation is sealed against condensation. In practice a villa plant cupboard sits next to the pool room or directly below it, and a hotel plant room serves the plunge pool alongside the other pools on the same level.

What does the plant space need?

  • Air and heat: the plant gives off heat. A cupboard needs ventilation or extract; an air-cooled chiller needs its own outside position with free air in and out, as its maker sets out.
  • Water and drain: a floor drain under the plant and a hose point, so a leak or a backwash never reaches finished floors.
  • Access: a door wide enough to change the chiller, and room to open the filter and the panel.
  • Sound: pumps and chillers hum. Keep them away from bedrooms and treatment rooms, and stand them on anti-vibration mounts.
  • A lock: treatment chemicals and electrics are not for guests or children.

How does a villa differ from a hotel?

In a villa, the plant usually fits a cupboard next to the pool room, with the chiller on a shaded terrace or roof. Sound and access matter most, because the plant is close to bedrooms and is serviced by visiting technicians. In a hotel, the plunge pool shares a plant room with the other pools, and the questions are about separate circuits, clear labels and a route for staff that never crosses guest areas. Frequently asked questions

Can the chiller sit inside the plant cupboard?

Only a water-cooled unit, or an air-cooled unit with ducted air sized by its maker. An air-cooled chiller in a closed cupboard overheats.

Does the plant have to be on the same floor as the pool?

No, but the pump is happiest at or below the pool water level. Plant above the pool needs the pump maker's approval.

Who decides the plant position?

We propose it at the site visit and draw it with the pool; the MEP engineer confirms power, drain and ventilation.

How much space does the plant need?

It depends on the pool volume and the equipment chosen. We give the plant footprint and service clearances with the quote, from the makers' data.

What warranty is given?

24 months on workmanship and manufacturing, 24 months manufacturer's warranty on electrical and mechanical equipment, and 5 years on structure and waterproofing, on every project.
