A Turkish Hammam: Where the Plant Goes

Updated: 5 November 2026

Short answer: most of a Turkish hammam's plant is heating plant. The heated marble platform, the floor and sometimes the walls are warmed by an electric mat or a hot water loop, and the basins need hot water on demand. The manifold, pumps, mixing valves, any steam generator, the extract fan and the controls go in a plant cupboard beside the hammam, never under the marble. Sauna Dekor Spa Solutions draws this cupboard on the first plan, because the heating pipes and cables all lead to it.

What plant does a Turkish hammam have?

A steam room is heated by steam alone. A Turkish hammam is heated mostly through its surfaces, so the list is longer:

  • heating for the platform, the floor zones and the bench, each on its own circuit or loop;
  • a hot water source for the basins and the hand showers;
  • mixing valves that set the basin water;
  • in many projects, a steam generator that lifts the humidity;
  • an extract fan for drying after use;
  • the controls and sensors for each heating zone.

Electric mat or water loop: does it change the plant?

Yes. An electric mat needs only a controller, a contactor and a protected circuit per zone, so the cupboard is small. A water loop needs a heat source, a pump, a manifold with a valve per loop, an expansion vessel and a way to fill and drain the circuit. It suits hammams where the villa or hotel already has a boiler or heat pump. In a hotel the hammam usually takes heat from the building system through a heat exchanger, so a leak in the hammam loop never drains the hotel circuit.

Either way, the sensor sits inside the platform and its cable or capillary runs in a duct back to the cupboard, so it can be replaced without lifting the slab.

Where does the basin water come from?

From the nearest hot water source, through a mixing valve close to the basins. In a hammam with two or more basins we prefer a short recirculation loop, so the first water from the tap is already warm. The mixing valve sits where it can be reached: behind a panel on the basin wall or in the plant cupboard with short pipes to the taps. Basin sizes and spacing are set per project; the plant only has to deliver water where they are.

How far can the cupboard be from the room?

As close as the plan allows. Pump, manifold and steam generator makers each give limits for pipe length, height and bends in their documents, and we take the distances from those documents for the chosen equipment. We pair Condair generators with our rooms; the unit and its data are Condair's.

What changes between a villa and a hotel?

In a villa the cupboard is often shared with a sauna or steam room. It needs ventilation, a floor drain, a light and a door wide enough to change a pump. In a hotel the plant moves to a central room with longer runs, the controls go to a staff area, and the hammam loop is separated from the building circuit.

Frequently asked questions

Can the manifold go under the platform?

We do not put it there. Pipes and cables run under the platform; the manifold and the valves stay in the cupboard where they can be reached.

Does a Turkish hammam always need a steam generator?

No. Many run on surface heating and hot water alone. A generator is added when the owner wants more humidity.

Is the plant noisy?

Pumps are quiet on anti-vibration mounts. The fan is what people hear, so its position matters.
