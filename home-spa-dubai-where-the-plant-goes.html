A Home Spa in Dubai: Where the Plant Goes

Updated: 6 November 2026

Short answer: at Sauna Dekor Spa Solutions we place the plant on the first layout, before the rooms are fixed, because moving it later moves pipes, cables and ceilings. In a Dubai home spa, the sauna heater sits in the room but its power unit sits outside it; the steam generator sits in a ventilated cupboard close to the steam room; the cold plunge chiller and any pool or jacuzzi filtration sit where they can reject heat and be serviced; the water softener sits on the incoming line before all of them. Each item needs service access, a drain where it handles water, and a place where its noise and heat do not reach a bedroom.

Distances and clearances come from each maker's installation manual. This guide gives the logic; the manual gives the numbers.

Why does the plant need a place before the rooms?

Because every room depends on something outside it. A sauna needs a heater circuit and a contactor or power unit. A steam room needs a generator, a water feed, a drain and a steam line. A cold plunge needs a chiller and a filter. In a Dubai villa these usually share one plant space; in an apartment they are spread between cupboards and the balcony. If the rooms are drawn first, the plant ends up wherever there is space left, and that is usually too far, too hot or too close to a bedroom.

Where does the sauna's control equipment go?

The heater stays in the sauna. Its contactor or power unit goes outside the hot room, on a dry wall nearby, with a removable panel in front of it. The control panel goes outside the door or on the wall inside, as the model allows. The temperature sensor goes where the maker's manual puts it, usually above the heater.

We have used EOS heaters for about 20 years; the power units, sensors and controls come with the heater and their positions come from its manual.

Where does the steam generator go?

Close to the steam room, in a ventilated cupboard or the plant room, with the steam line running to the room within the length the maker allows and falling back towards the generator. The generator needs a water feed with a valve, a drain for its blow-down, and air around it. The steam room itself runs at 40–50 °C at close to 100 % relative humidity, so nothing from the generator side should sit inside it apart from the steam outlet and the sensor.

Where do the chiller and filtration go?

We build cold plunge pools for water temperatures from 2 to 15 °C. The chiller that holds that temperature gives off heat, and in a Dubai summer it must reject it into air that can carry it away: an open plant yard, a ventilated plant room or a roof. A spa pool or jacuzzi at about 30 °C has its own pump, filter and heater, which can share the same space.

Both need a floor drain, a valve on every line and room to pull the filter out.

What changes between a villa and an apartment?

A villa usually has a plant room or a yard, so the plant can sit together. An apartment rarely has either. There, the steam generator goes in a ventilated utility cupboard, the chiller goes on a balcony or in a space the building management allows, and noise to the neighbours is checked before anything is ordered. Building rules on balcony equipment and drains apply first.

How are noise and heat kept away from the house?

Chillers and pumps run while the family sleeps, so the plant does not share a wall with a bedroom where it can be avoided. Anti-vibration mounts go under pumps and chillers. Plant rooms get their own extract to outside, never into the villa's cooled air.

Frequently asked questions

Can all the plant go in one room?

In most villas, yes, if that room is ventilated and within each maker's distance limits from the rooms it serves.

Can the steam generator go in the loft?

Only if the maker's manual allows the line length and the drain can fall from it. Most lofts are too hot for it.

Does a sauna need a plant room?

No. It needs a dry wall nearby for its power unit and a circuit from the board.

Who decides the distances?

The equipment makers, in their manuals. We draw the layout from those values.
