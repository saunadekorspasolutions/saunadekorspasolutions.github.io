Sauna room size for a UAE villa: volume, benches and heater

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Short answer: most villa saunas we build in the UAE seat 2 to 6 people and are sized around the bench, not the floor plan. A room for 4 people with two bench levels and a ceiling of about 2.1 m comes to roughly 8–10 m³. Each square metre of glass then adds about 1.2 m³ to the volume the heater must be sized for.

Why do you size a sauna from the bench?

Because the bench decides where people sit in the heat. A traditional Finnish sauna runs at 80–100 °C and a bio sauna at 50–65 °C, and in both the air is hottest under the ceiling. The upper bench therefore sits high, and the ceiling is kept low enough that the heat stays at head height instead of collecting in an empty space above it. Floor area follows from bench length and depth.

These are typical practice values for villa rooms, not fixed rules. A bench seat needs about 60 cm of width per person sitting, and a lying bench needs at least the full height of the person. The upper bench is usually placed about 1.0–1.1 m below the ceiling, so a person sitting on it has the warmest air at face level.

How does ceiling height change the volume?

Directly. Volume is length × width × ceiling height, and the heater is sized for that volume. A 2.0 × 1.8 m room with a 2.1 m ceiling is 7.6 m³; the same room with a 2.4 m ceiling is 8.6 m³, about 14 % more air to heat for no extra seat. In villas with high slabs we usually lower the sauna ceiling with a timber-clad soffit rather than heat the void.

How does glass change the heater size?

Glass, stone and concrete surfaces absorb heat that timber would hold, so they are counted as extra volume. Harvia's rule is about +1.2 m³ for each square metre of glass, stone or concrete; Tylö and HUUM use about 1 m³. A 2.0 × 1.8 × 2.1 m room with a 2 m² glass front is therefore sized as roughly 10 m³, not 7.6 m³. Heater makers publish their own tables, and the final heater is chosen from the maker's table for the corrected volume.

What does the room need from the building?

Four things, all drawn before the walls close:

  1. Power: a dedicated supply for the heater and control panel, sized from the heater datasheet.
  2. Ventilation: a fresh-air inlet low near the heater and an outlet on the opposite side.
  3. Floor: a finish that tolerates heat and water, with a drain if the room is washed down.
  4. Door: opening outwards, with a gap at the bottom for air.

In Dubai, Abu Dhabi and across the UAE the sauna often shares a wing with a steam room or a cold plunge, so the plant positions are agreed for the whole wing at once.

How long does a villa sauna take?

For a single sauna, delivery takes about 2 weeks once the room is ready and the drawings are fixed. Larger home spa wings with several rooms take longer and are planned as one project.
